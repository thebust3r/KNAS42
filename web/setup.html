    👀 View only

    Settings can only be changed from a device on the same home network as KNAS. You're looking from outside it.

    Where are you? Needed for weather

    Your town is used for the weather, the forecast, the radar map and the sunrise and sunset times. If you don't set one, KNAS guesses it from your internet connection. Everything else has a sensible default.

    📍 KNAS guessed your town

    From your internet connection it looks like you're in , so the weather is already working. If that's right, there's nothing to do. If it's wrong (internet providers sometimes place you in a nearby city), type your town below and press Save.
    How does this work?
    Press Find and pick your town from the list. That saves its exact location, and the time zone fills itself in. If you skip Find, KNAS looks the town up by itself when it starts. Add the state or country if your town's name is common.
    Units
    Why does it need a time zone?
    The recorded weather channel and break clips show a clock. The time zone makes sure it matches your local time. It uses names like America/New_York, America/Chicago, America/Denver, America/Los_Angeles or Europe/London.

    Channels & screen Optional

    Where your KNAS channels sit in the cable box (/tv), and how the screens fit your TV.

    Channel numbers

    Use 0 to hide a channel. If you pick a number one of your ErsatzTV channels already uses, the KNAS channel takes that spot.

    Phone extras

    Fun things phones on your Wi-Fi can do with the TVs. Each one is off until you turn it on. Live Cam, Duck Season and the voice remote use KNAS's secure address (https://this-nas:8645), because phones only share the camera, microphone and motion sensors with secure pages. The first time a phone opens it, it warns that the connection "isn't private": that's KNAS's own certificate, so tap Show details → visit this website (iPhone) or Advanced → Proceed (Android), once per phone.
    📤 Throw it to the TV
    Pick a photo or video on your phone at /throw (or 📤 on the remote) and flick it up: it flies onto the TV. Photos stay up 30 seconds; videos play to the end. BACK closes it. Thrown files are deleted after a day.
    🍅 Screen fun
    From a phone at /fun (☰ on the remote): flick a tomato, pie, egg or snowball and it splats on the TV; shake your phones (or tap) for confetti and fireworks; tap emojis to float them up the screen; or draw over the picture like a football telestrator. It shows over whatever's on.
    🎤 Voice remote
    Adds a mic button to the phone remote. Say "channel 42", "put on Nickelodeon", "King of the Hill", "volume up", "last channel" or "play Thriller on the jukebox". Your phone's own speech recognition does the listening (Apple's or Google's, like dictating a text).
    📹 Live Cam
    Any phone's camera becomes a live channel with a news-style "LIVE • KITCHEN" banner. Up to 4 at once. Phones go to /cam (📹 on the remote). Pictures only, no sound, and nothing is recorded.
    🎯 Flick games
    Darts, hoops, bowling and cup pong for up to 6 players. Phones go to /flick; flick on the phone and it flies on the TV. Which way and how hard you flick decide the throw. In bowling, bend your swipe to curve the ball.
    📡 Stream channel
    Paste a live stream link on a phone at /streams (☰ on the remote) and it plays on this channel: YouTube live streams, Twitch, news and TV station sites, or a direct .m3u8 link. Save favorites for one tap. It uses the jukebox's video tool (set up by itself the first time). Paid apps like Netflix or ESPN+ lock their video, so they can't play here.
    🦆 Duck Season
    A light-gun game: your phone is the gun (up to 4 players, at /zapper). Zapper mode works like the old light guns: the screen flashes black with white boxes where the ducks are, and the phone's camera checks if you were really aimed at one. Tilt mode moves a crosshair with the phone's motion sensors instead. Heads up: zapper shots flash the screen black and white.

    Party games channel optional, off until you give it a number

    Party games on the TV, played on everyone's phones. Everyone scans the code on screen (or opens /p) and types a name. The first one in is the host and picks the game: Trivia (Open Trivia Database topics and your own list), Name That Tune (a few seconds of a jukebox song, sound only), Word Race (everyone solves the same five-letter word), Bluff (make up fake answers to strange-but-true facts), Wheel Puzzle (spin, call letters, solve) and Draw It (one person draws on their phone, everyone guesses). Leaderboard between rounds, podium at the end.

    Jukebox channel optional, off until you give it a number

    A channel that plays YouTube videos (music videos at a party, say). Anyone on your Wi-Fi opens /jukebox on their phone, pastes a YouTube link, and it joins the list. Every TV on the channel plays the same video, and the next one starts by itself.
    Download the videos so official music videos play too
    Many music videos are blocked from playing outside YouTube. With this on, the NAS downloads each video in the list ahead of time (720p, using the free yt-dlp tool, which KNAS sets up and keeps updated by itself), and the TVs play it from the NAS: no blocks, no ads, every TV in sync. A song that was downloaded before is never downloaded again. Downloading from YouTube goes against YouTube's terms, so it's your call.
    The jukebox channel shows a QR code guests scan with their phone camera to open the song page (or they can type /j). Fill in your Wi-Fi here and it also shows a code that joins guests to your Wi-Fi first. Leave it blank to skip that. Use a guest network if you'd rather not show your main password; the jukebox works from any Wi-Fi that can reach your NAS.
    A video says YouTube asked KNAS to sign in, or it's age-restricted
    Once in a while YouTube wants proof you're a person. On your computer, install a "cookies.txt" browser extension (like Get cookies.txt LOCALLY), open youtube.com signed in, export the cookies, name the file youtube-cookies.txt and put it in the data folder inside your KNAS folder. KNAS uses it from then on. Treat that file like a password.
    Good to know
    It plays in the cable box (/tv) on TVs, computers and phones that can open YouTube. It can't be one of the recorded channels that go to ErsatzTV. Some official music videos are blocked from playing outside YouTube; KNAS tells you when you add one, or skips it. YouTube may show its own ads. If a TV starts it with no sound, press any button on that TV.

    Screen

    Screen shape
    Which one should I pick?
    Fill any screen stretches the layout, not the picture, to fit widescreen TVs and phones. Classic 4:3 keeps the old square shape with black bars, which is best for a CRT. TV shows always keep their own shape either way. You can also pick per device by adding ?screen=4:3 or ?screen=auto to the address.
    Channel up goes to…
    For the CH ▲ / ▼ buttons, a remote's channel buttons, the up and down arrows and the mouse wheel while you're watching. Phone swipes always go up for the next channel. The next higher number works like a real TV remote. The guide always scrolls the normal way. You can also set it per device by adding ?chup=number or ?chup=guide to the address.
    Order of screens weather pages, sports teams and break segments
    With shuffle on, the weather channel still starts with Current Conditions and breaks still start with "We'll Be Right Back".

    Viewing history

    KNAS can remember what each TV watched: which channels, which shows and for how long. See it on the viewing history page. It stays on your NAS and nothing leaves your home.
    KNAS Wrapped is your year in TV, like Spotify Wrapped: top shows, top channels, your biggest binge and your TV personality. Open it any time at /wrapped, or give it a channel number and it plays on a loop in the cable box.

    How do I list my own towns?
    Write Name, latitude, longitude for each town, with a semicolon between towns. You can get the numbers by right-clicking a spot in Google Maps. Leave it blank and KNAS uses the biggest towns near you from OpenStreetMap.

    Music

    Severe weather alerts optional, US only, off until you turn it on

    When the National Weather Service puts out a warning for your town (a tornado warning, say), every TV with the cable box open shows the classic red emergency crawl over whatever's on. A small ⚠ tag stays in the corner until it ends. Press Back to clear the crawl.
    Which alerts
    Warnings mean it's happening or about to (tornado, severe thunderstorm, flash flood, blizzard…). Watches mean conditions are right for it. Advisories are smaller things like wind, fog or frost.

    Score bug

    A tiny score box in the corner of whatever's on (a show, a movie, the weather) while one of your teams is playing. It flashes when your team scores.
    On and off: shows for a few minutes, hides for a while, then comes back. It always pops up when your team scores and at the final.

    News headlines Optional

    One news source per line. Each line can be a news website (like kshb.com), a few words to search (like Kansas City Chiefs), or an RSS feed. Press Check to see the headlines it finds.

    Older stories are skipped, so the news is always current. If a site's own feed has nothing that recent, KNAS gets that site's newest stories from Google News instead.
    How does it get headlines from a regular website?
    First it looks for the site's own headline feed. If the site doesn't have one, or the feed hasn't been updated in weeks, KNAS gets that site's latest headlines from Google News instead, so they're always current. Typing words instead of a website searches Google News for them. The ticker shows one headline from each list, and the News pages show the top ones.

    Facts & trivia Optional

    The "Did You Know?" facts and trivia questions on the break screen, and the facts in the weather channel ticker. Mix your own lists with facts and trivia pulled from the internet.

    Facts

    What can I put here?
    • A topic in plain words, like Space, Dinosaurs, The Simpsons or Chicago. KNAS pulls facts from its Wikipedia article.
    • A Wikipedia page link.
    • A Google Sheet with one fact per row in the first column. In Google Sheets use Share → Anyone with the link.
    • A text file link (.txt or .csv) with one fact per line.
    • Any web page with a list of facts, or an RSS feed.
    They're fetched every few hours and saved, so the break screen never waits on the internet.

    Trivia

    Trivia topics from the free Open Trivia Database, pick any
    How do I make my own trivia sheet?
    One question per row: question in the first column, the right answer in the second, and up to three wrong answers after that. A text file works the same, with | between them:
    What year did Toy Story come out? | 1995 | 1993 | 1997 | 1999

    Jellyfin or Emby optional

    If ErsatzTV gets your shows from Jellyfin (or Emby), add it here. Then whenever the commercial breaks tool adds breaks to an episode, KNAS tells Jellyfin to re-read just that episode, so the breaks reach ErsatzTV by themselves. No more refreshing shows by hand.
    How do I get an API key?
    Jellyfin: Dashboard → API Keys → +, name it KNAS, and copy the key.
    Emby: Settings → Advanced → API Keys → New API Key.
    ErsatzTV picks up the change on its next library sync from Jellyfin (every few hours by default; ErsatzTV's library refresh interval setting changes that). To see it right away, sync the library under Media Sources → Jellyfin in ErsatzTV.

    Sound Optional

    Every channel, song and radio station is recorded at a different volume. The volume leveler evens them out as they play, so you're not grabbing the remote every time you change channels.

    Volume leveler

    More sound settings are under Show advanced settings at the bottom of the menu.
    Strength
    Which strength should I pick?
    Light only smooths out the jumps between channels and keeps movies sounding natural. Medium also tames loud scenes and commercials, and is a good everyday choice. Strong keeps whispers and explosions close together, like a TV's night mode. It's great for late nights, but music can sound flatter.
    More leveler options
    Limiter a ceiling nothing can get louder than

    Volume of each kind of sound

    Match these by ear. 100% is normal.
    Where does leveling work?
    • KNAS Cable box (/tv) on TV boxes, PCs, Xbox and Android phones: everything is leveled.
    • Recorded channels (weather stream and break clips for ErsatzTV): leveled too, when the leveler is on.
    • iPhones and iPads: Apple doesn't let web pages adjust video sound, so TV channels play at their normal volume there.
    • Shows watched straight in Jellyfin or the ErsatzTV app don't go through KNAS. Turn on Normalize Loudness in ErsatzTV's FFmpeg profile for those.
    • Leveling starts as soon as the page is allowed to play sound. On some devices that's after the first button press.

    Getting the clips to ErsatzTV

    Set your ErsatzTV address first, under ErsatzTV, and say where ErsatzTV runs. The steps for your setup will show up here.
    ErsatzTV runs on this same machine, so it can read the clips straight from the break-clips folder inside your KNAS folder. Nothing gets copied.
    1. In your NAS's Docker app, edit the ErsatzTV container (or its compose file) and add a volume / folder mapping:
      your KNAS folder's break-clips folder → /knas-clips (read-only is fine).
      Compose example: - /volume1/docker/knas-retro-tv/break-clips:/knas-clips:ro (use your real KNAS folder path).
    2. Restart the ErsatzTV container.
    3. In ErsatzTV: Media Sources → Local, add a library of kind Other Videos with the folder /knas-clips, and scan it.
    4. Make the filler presets (see How do I use break clips in ErsatzTV? below).
    ErsatzTV runs on a Windows PC, which can't read the clips from the NAS by itself. One small setup file takes care of it: it copies the clips onto the PC and keeps them fresh by itself.
    Download Get KNAS Break Clips.bat
    1. On the PC that runs ErsatzTV, download the file above and double-click it. It already knows this NAS's address. (If Windows warns you, click More info → Run anyway, or Keep in your browser.)
    2. It sets everything up and shows you your clips folder (usually C:\Users\you\KNAS Break Clips\break-clips). The folder path is already copied, so you can paste it.
    3. In ErsatzTV: Media Sources → Local, add a library of kind Other Videos, paste that folder, save, and scan it.
    4. Put that library's number in Keep ErsatzTV up to date below, then make the filler presets (see How do I use break clips in ErsatzTV?).
    It checks for new or missing clips every 5 minutes in the background (no windows pop up), updates itself when KNAS updates, and gives KNAS ErsatzTV's API key by itself. Running the file again is safe. The Copied to ErsatzTV computer column above shows it working.
    ErsatzTV runs on a Mac or Linux computer. A small copier keeps a fresh copy of the clips on that computer.
    Download the copier (.sh)
    1. Put knas-clip-sync.sh in a folder on that computer. It already knows this NAS's address.
    2. Open Terminal in that folder and run it once: sh knas-clip-sync.sh. The clips land in a break-clips folder next to it.
    3. Make it run every 5 minutes: run crontab -e and add the line
      */5 * * * * /bin/sh "/full/path/to/knas-clip-sync.sh" >/dev/null 2>&1
    4. In ErsatzTV: add a Local library of kind Other Videos with that break-clips folder, scan it, and make the filler presets (below).

    Keep ErsatzTV up to date

    ErsatzTV only notices new clips when it scans its library. Until then it can try to play a clip it remembers and say the media isn't available. Give KNAS the library's number and it asks ErsatzTV to rescan the clips every time they change.
    Where do I find the number?
    In ErsatzTV, go to Media Sources → Local and click the edit (pencil) button on the library that has the KNAS break clips. Look at the address bar: the number in it is the library number, for example …/media/sources/local/5/edit means 5. Press Test: ErsatzTV should start scanning that library.
    Newer ErsatzTV versions (26.8 and later) need their API key. If ErsatzTV is on a Windows PC, Mac or Linux computer, the clip copier finds the key and fills it in for you the next time it runs. Just wait for its next copy, or run it once by hand.
    Where do I find the API key myself?
    You only need this if ErsatzTV runs in Docker on the NAS, or the copier couldn't find the key. ErsatzTV saves the key in a file called api-secrets.json in its config folder:
    • Windows: press Windows key + R, type %LOCALAPPDATA%\ersatztv and press Enter.
    • Docker: the folder you mapped to ErsatzTV's config (often /config or /root/.local/share/ersatztv inside the container).
    Open api-secrets.json with Notepad or any text editor. Copy the long key (the text between the quotes, not the name before it) and paste it here. Older ErsatzTV versions don't need a key: leave it blank.

    Clip settings

    They're also re-made just after midnight. Every 3 hours keeps the headlines in them current and the trivia and facts mixed up.
    How do I use break clips in ErsatzTV?
    1) Get the clips to ErsatzTV (see Getting the clips to ErsatzTV above), add them as a Local library of kind Other Videos, scan it, and make a collection of the clips.
    2) In ErsatzTV, go to Lists → Filler Presets.
    3) Create a Filler Preset: Post-Roll, Mode Pad, Pad to Nearest Minute 30, using that collection. Create another: Fallback, same collection.
    4) On your schedule items set Start Type Dynamic, Post-Roll = the Pad preset, Fallback = the Fallback preset, then reset the playouts.

    Common questions

    How do I watch on my TV?

    Open http:///tv in any browser. For a TV box (Onn, Chromecast, Fire TV), install Fully Kiosk Browser, set its start page to that address, and turn on Autoplay Videos and Launch on Boot. On an Xbox, use Edge. A game controller works as a remote.

    How do I use the remote?
    • Up / Down: change channel
    • OK: TV guide (OK again to tune)
    • Left: last channel
    • Right: what's on
    • Back: close
    • Phone: swipe up or down for channels, tap for info, double-tap for the guide
    • Controller: LB/RB for channels, A for guide, B for back
    How do I make it full screen on a phone?

    iPhone: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app with no address bar. Tapping a TV channel in Safari also opens it in the iPhone player. Android: the first tap goes full screen, and ⋮ → Add to Home screen makes it an app.

    It's choppy or laggy on my TV box.

    Set the box's output resolution to 720p or 1080p instead of 4K in its display settings. KNAS also turns off its heavier effects by itself on Android TV boxes.

    The picture looks squished or skinny on my CRT.

    Pick Classic 4:3 under Screen shape. If it still looks skinny, add ?stretch=1 to the address. If the edges are cut off, add ?zoom=0.92.

    The words look blocky or blurry.

    Try the Smooth or Modern News font under Fonts, and set Text shadow to Soft. On a TV box, setting its output to 1080p instead of 4K also makes text sharper.

    KNAS is using a lot of CPU on my NAS.

    That's the recorder (the knas42-cast container), which runs a hidden web browser and a video encoder. Under Breaks & clips, untick Weather channel stream unless you added the weather channel to ErsatzTV. The break screen only runs for a few minutes each time clips are made. If you don't use ErsatzTV at all, untick both and also blank out Clips to make. Lowering Smoothness to 15 fps under Recording quality helps too.

    A page is missing or empty.

    Optional pages hide when they have nothing to show. To see why, open http:///api/all and look at the errors section.

    How do updates work?

    Drop a new KNAS .zip into the updates folder. It installs itself, keeps your settings, teams, facts, trivia and music, and restarts. updates/update-log.txt shows what happened, and a broken update is refused.

    Will saving here mess anything up?

    No. Values that don't make sense are skipped and your old setting is kept. Settings that aren't on this page are left alone. Every save also keeps a copy of your previous settings in data/settings.backup.txt. If something goes wrong, copy that file over settings.txt.

    Can I edit the settings by hand instead?

    Yes. Everything here is saved in settings.txt and teams.txt in the KNAS folder. Edit them with any text editor and KNAS restarts itself within a few seconds of saving.

    Is this private? Can other people change my settings?

    KNAS only runs on your home network. Only devices on that network can save changes here. The only things it contacts on the internet are free weather, map, news and sports data services, with no accounts or tracking.

    Where does all the data come from?

    Weather: Open-Meteo. Radar: RainViewer. Maps and towns: OpenStreetMap. Scores: ESPN's public scoreboard. News: the feeds you add, or Google News. On This Day: Wikipedia. All free, no keys needed. Fonts are free (SIL Open Font License).
