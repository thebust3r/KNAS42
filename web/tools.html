How do I copy a folder's location?
  • Windows: open the folder in File Explorer, click the bar at the top that shows the folder's name, and copy. It looks like \\192.168.1.50\Media\TV or Z:\TV.
  • Mac: in Finder, right-click the folder, hold Option and choose Copy "TV" as Pathname, or use its smb:// address.
  • Your NAS's Files app: open the folder's info or properties and copy its location, like /volume1/Media/TV.

Add your shows

KNAS can't see your NAS's drive yet. This takes one change, once:
  1. Open your NAS's Docker app → Project → your KNAS project → Edit (the docker-compose.yml).
  2. Under knas42: → volumes:, add this line under - ./:/knas: 
          - /volume1:/nas/volume1
    That's for UGREEN and Synology. On QNAP use - /share:/nas/share, on Unraid - /mnt/user:/nas/user.
  3. Save and redeploy the project, then refresh this page.

Auto-mark this folder

Works with any video type. Files that can't hold breaks (.avi, .wmv, .ts, .mpg and so on) are saved as .mkv with the same picture and sound. Episodes whose own chapters already sit at the act breaks are left alone. Chapters that are junk (bunched at the start, doubled up) get replaced. Change

This runs on the NAS, not in your browser, so you can close this page or turn off your computer. It keeps going, and if the NAS restarts it carries on where it left off. A big library takes a while (often about a minute per episode), and it runs at low priority so the TV stays smooth.
