# --- Weather ---
Lightning is about five times hotter than the surface of the sun.
The fastest wind gust ever measured outside a tornado was 253 mph, on Barrow Island, Australia, in 1996.
The coldest temperature ever recorded on Earth was -128.6°F at Vostok Station, Antarctica, in 1983.
Count the seconds between lightning and thunder, then divide by five. That's roughly how many miles away the strike was.
The largest hailstone on record in the U.S. fell in Vivian, South Dakota, in 2010. It was 8 inches across.
Fog is just a cloud that's touching the ground.
Thunderstorm clouds can tower more than 10 miles into the sky.
An average puffy cumulus cloud holds about a million pounds of water.
Mount Washington in New Hampshire held the world wind record of 231 mph for 62 years.
The Weather Channel first went on the air on May 2, 1982.
On The Weather Channel, local forecasts aired at times ending in 8, known as "Local on the 8s."
Lightning strikes somewhere on Earth about 44 times every second.
Rainbows are full circles. The ground usually hides the bottom half.
The earthy smell after rain on dry ground has a name: petrichor.
Falling air pressure on a barometer usually means stormy weather is on the way.
When the dew point climbs above 65°F, the air starts to feel muggy.
The 1925 Tri-State Tornado traveled about 219 miles across Missouri, Illinois and Indiana.
Hurricane names get retired when a storm is especially deadly or destructive.
At any given moment, around 2,000 thunderstorms are happening around the world.
Hailstones grow in layers as updrafts toss them up and down inside a storm, like rings in a tree.
Sleet is rain that freezes into ice pellets before it hits the ground. Freezing rain freezes after it lands.
The eye of a hurricane is often calm, with light winds and sometimes blue sky.
Tornado Alley isn't an official region. It's a nickname for the central plains where tornadoes are common.

Crater of Diamonds State Park in Murfreesboro is the only diamond site in the world open to the public, and you keep what you find.
Hot Springs was set aside by Congress in 1832, making it the oldest federally protected area in the country.
The Buffalo River became America's first National River in 1972.

# --- 90s, TV & games ---
The original PlayStation launched in North America on September 9, 1995.
The Nintendo 64 launched in North America on September 29, 1996.
The Xbox 360 launched in North America on November 22, 2005.
Toy Story, released in 1995, was the first feature film made entirely with computer animation.
Titanic was the first movie to earn more than $1 billion worldwide.
The Seinfeld finale aired on May 14, 1998, and was watched by about 76 million people.
Pokémon Red and Blue arrived in North America in September 1998.
The Tamagotchi virtual pet first went on sale in Japan in 1996.
Furby was the must-have toy of the 1998 holiday season.
"Macarena" spent 14 weeks at number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1996.
Blockbuster Video peaked at about 9,000 stores in 2004. The last one left is in Bend, Oregon.
A CRT TV draws its picture with a beam of electrons sweeping across the screen line by line.
American analog TV used 525 lines, drawn about 30 times every second.
Color TV in the U.S. runs at 29.97 frames per second instead of 30 so the color signal wouldn't interfere with the sound.
Full-power analog TV broadcasts in the U.S. ended on June 12, 2009.
The NES Zapper light gun only works on CRT TVs. It relies on the exact timing of the screen's scan.
The "You've got mail!" voice on AOL belonged to Elwood Edwards.
Microsoft paid to use the Rolling Stones' "Start Me Up" to launch Windows 95.
The Simpsons premiered as a half-hour series on December 17, 1989.
The first text message ever sent said "Merry Christmas." It was sent on December 3, 1992.
The DVD format launched in the United States in 1997.
Google was founded in 1998, in a garage in Menlo Park, California.
Amazon started out in 1994 as an online bookstore.
The official website for the 1996 movie Space Jam stayed online, nearly unchanged, for decades.
Super Mario 64 was one of the first games to give players full 3D movement with an analog stick.
Before remotes were common, changing the channel meant getting up and turning a dial on the TV.
VHS tapes in "SP" mode held two hours. "EP" mode squeezed in six hours at lower quality.
Old TVs had separate VHF and UHF dials. Channels 2 through 13 were VHF.

# --- Science & nature ---
Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth.
Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a storm wider than the whole Earth.
Saturn is less dense than water. In a big enough bathtub, it would float.
Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood.
Bananas count as berries, but strawberries don't.
The Eiffel Tower grows about six inches taller in summer as the metal expands in the heat.
Sharks have been around longer than trees.
Wombats make cube-shaped poop.
Sea otters sometimes hold hands while they sleep so they don't drift apart.
A group of crows is called a murder.
Archaeologists have found pots of honey in Egyptian tombs that were thousands of years old and still edible.
Your body has about as many bacteria cells as human cells.
Hummingbirds are the only birds that can fly backwards.
The Great Wall of China isn't visible to the naked eye from space.
There are more possible ways to shuffle a deck of cards than there are atoms on Earth.
